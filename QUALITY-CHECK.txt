CHOTU FRAME JOURNAL
===================

Asset and content checks
------------------------
- 30 WebP photographs are stored beside index.html.
- All photo data references resolve to the original repository-root files.
- Six collections are defined; their displayed frame counts total 30.
- Public contact details use chotu.framejournal@gmail.com and
  @chotu.framejournal.
- The portfolio identity is Chotu Frame Journal, with Vishal credited
  personally as the photographer behind the journal.
- No em dash characters are used in the website copy.

Gallery layout
--------------
- The archive uses a conventional responsive CSS Grid.
- Every archive thumbnail has a fixed 4:5 image window. Images are cropped
  inside the window with object-fit: cover.
- The browser reserves the full thumbnail window before a lazy image loads.
- There is no JavaScript masonry sizing, row-span calculation, ResizeObserver,
  or image-load-driven layout change.
- Selecting a thumbnail opens the original full-size photograph in the
  existing lightbox, without the archive crop.

Performance
-----------
- Archive images remain lazy-loaded, asynchronously decoded and low priority.
- The repository still stores full-size original images. Lightweight
  derivative thumbnails should be generated from the original assets as a
  separate optimization step.
