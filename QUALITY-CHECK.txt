CHOTU FRAME JOURNAL
===================

Content and identity
--------------------
- The public identity is Chotu Frame Journal.
- The homepage presents the archive as a personal point of view.
- The About section introduces Vishal, also known as Chotu.
- Public contact details use chotu.framejournal@gmail.com and
  @chotu.framejournal.
- All 30 photographs have individual, scene-specific captions.
- No em dash characters are used in visible site copy.

Photo assets and performance
----------------------------
- 30 original WebP photographs remain at the repository root.
- GitHub Actions generates 960px maximum-side WebP thumbnails at quality 72.
- Archive and collection cards use thumbnail files.
- The lightbox continues to use original full-resolution photographs.
- Actual image dimensions are read from each original file during generation.
  Width and height attributes reserve each image's natural ratio before
  its thumbnail downloads.
- Archive images are lazy-loaded, asynchronously decoded and low priority.

Layout
------
- The archive uses a conventional responsive CSS Grid.
- There is no JavaScript masonry sizing, ResizeObserver, or image-load-driven
  card repositioning.
- Responsive breakpoints are defined for desktop, tablet and mobile.
- Collection filters and keyboard-accessible photograph viewing remain.

Final browser checks
--------------------
- Scroll desktop and mobile archives while thumbnails load.
- Check that no cards jump or overlap.
- Check hero title placement and wrapping over the open sky.
- Check all captions and contact links before production merge.
