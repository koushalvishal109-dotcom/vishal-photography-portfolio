CHOTU FRAME JOURNAL
===================

Content and identity
--------------------
- The public identity is Chotu Frame Journal.
- The homepage introduces the work as a personal point of view.
- The About section introduces Vishal, also known as Chotu.
- Public contact details use chotu.framejournal@gmail.com and
  @chotu.framejournal.
- All 30 photographs have individual captions written for the image shown.
- No em dash characters are used in visible site copy.

Photo assets
------------
- 30 original WebP photographs remain at the repository root.
- GitHub Actions generates smaller 960px maximum-side WebP thumbnails
  in /thumbnails at quality 72.
- The archive and collection cards use thumbnails.
- The lightbox continues to use the original full-resolution photographs.
- Each photo record stores its original width and height, so the browser
  reserves the image's natural aspect ratio before the thumbnail downloads.

Layout and performance
----------------------
- The archive uses a conventional responsive CSS Grid.
- There is no JavaScript masonry sizing, ResizeObserver, or image-load-driven
  card repositioning.
- Archive images are lazy-loaded, asynchronously decoded and low priority.
- Responsive breakpoints are defined for desktop, tablet and mobile.
- Collection filters and keyboard-accessible photograph viewing remain.

Final browser checks
--------------------
- Scroll the desktop and mobile archive while thumbnails load.
- Check that no cards jump or overlap.
- Check headline placement, wrapping and readability over the hero image.
- Check every caption and contact link before the production merge.
